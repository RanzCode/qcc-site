Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about giving

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes, all donations to Quezon Central Church are tax-deductible. You will receive a receipt for your contributions at the end of the year.

How is my donation used?

Your donations support our church operations, ministry programs, community outreach, and mission projects. We are committed to financial transparency and good stewardship.

Can I designate my gift for a specific purpose?

Yes, you can designate your gift for a specific ministry or project. Simply indicate your preference when making your donation.

Is online giving secure?

Yes, our online giving platform uses industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal and financial information.
