What to Expect

New to our church? Here's what you can expect when you visit

When You Arrive

  • Friendly greeters will welcome you and help you find your way
  • Sabbath School classes for all ages
  • Children's programs during the service
  • Comfortable seating and a welcoming atmosphere

During Worship

  • Uplifting music and congregational singing
  • Bible-based sermon (approximately 40 minutes)
  • Prayer and scripture reading
  • Opportunity to connect with others after the service

We strive to create a welcoming environment for everyone. Dress comfortably - you'll find people in everything from casual to business attire.
