Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our church

What time are your worship services?

Our Saturday worship services begin with Sabbath School at 9:15 AM, followed by Divine Worship at 11:00 AM. We also have afternoon programs starting at 2:30 PM.

How do I become a member of the church?

We welcome new members through baptism, profession of faith, or transfer from another church. Please contact our church office or speak with our pastor for more information about membership.

Do you have programs for children?

Yes, we have age-appropriate Sabbath School classes for children of all ages, as well as youth programs and activities throughout the week.

How can I get involved in church activities?

There are many ways to get involved! You can join a ministry team, volunteer for community service, participate in Bible study groups, or help with various church programs. Contact our church office to learn about current opportunities.
