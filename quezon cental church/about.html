About Us

Learn about our history, mission, and the community that makes Quezon Central Church special.

Our Story

Quezon Central Church was founded in 1975 with a vision to create a welcoming community where people could grow in faith and serve others. What started as a small group of believers meeting in homes has grown into a vibrant congregation that serves the Quezon City community and beyond.

Over the decades, we have remained committed to our core values of faith, love, and service. Our church has been a beacon of hope in the community, providing spiritual guidance, community support, and outreach programs that make a real difference in people's lives.

Today, we continue to honor our heritage while embracing new ways to connect with our community and share God's love with all who enter our doors.

Our Mission

To glorify God by making disciples of Jesus Christ through worship, fellowship, discipleship, ministry, and evangelism, while serving our community with love and compassion.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered community that transforms lives, strengthens families, and impacts our city and world for the Kingdom of God.

Our Core Values

These values guide everything we do as a church community

Love

We believe love is the foundation of our faith and the driving force behind all our actions and relationships.

Service

We are called to serve others with humility and compassion, following Christ's example of selfless love.

Community

We value authentic relationships and believe that we grow stronger when we support one another in faith.

Growth

We are committed to continuous spiritual growth and helping others discover their purpose in God's plan.

Mission

We actively share the Gospel and serve our community, both locally and around the world.

Prayer

We believe in the power of prayer and seek God's guidance in all aspects of our lives and ministry.

Our Ministries

Discover the various ways we serve our community and grow together in faith

Children's Ministry

Age-appropriate programs that help children learn about God's love through fun activities, Bible stories, and fellowship.

Youth Ministry

Engaging programs for teenagers that focus on building strong Christian character and leadership skills.

Music Ministry

Our choir and musicians lead worship and provide special music that enhances our spiritual experience.

Community Outreach

Programs that serve our local community through food drives, assistance programs, and volunteer opportunities.

Bible Study Groups

Small group studies that provide opportunities for deeper fellowship and spiritual growth throughout the week.

Prayer Ministry

Dedicated prayer teams that intercede for our church, community, and world, believing in the power of prayer.

What We Believe

Our fundamental beliefs are rooted in Scripture and guide our faith and practice

The Trinity

We believe in one God eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Scripture

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our ultimate authority for faith and practice.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by works.

Second Coming

We believe in the personal, visible return of Jesus Christ to establish His eternal kingdom.
