## 📋 MISSION & VISION

### MISSION
At Quezon Central Church, our mission is to honor God by sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ and helping others grow in the faith of Jesus. We are committed to living and teaching the principles of the Ten Commandments, the Sanctuary message, and the Three Angels' Messages. Guided by Ellen G. White's writings through the Spirit of Prophecy, we strive to lead lives filled with compassion, worship, service, and love.

### VISION
We envision a Christ-centered, Spirit-led community that reflects God's love and prepares hearts for Jesus' soon return. Rooted in the Bible and strengthened by the message of prophecy, we aim to live out and share the truths of God's Word—shaping lives, families, and communities through the Gospel, the Ten Commandments, the Sanctuary doctrine, and the Three Angels' Messages.
